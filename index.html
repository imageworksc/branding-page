What Is Branding?

Branding is the complete system of how a business looks, sounds, and is remembered — its logo, colors, typography, voice, and the promise those elements make to a customer every time they show up.

It's bigger than a logo. A logo is one visual mark. Branding is the consistent identity that mark belongs to — applied across your website, print materials, social presence, and every other place your business shows up.
